Title: Moving to Lektor
Date: 2017-04-29 16:00:00
Author: Russell Keith-Magee

Almost nine years ago, I launched cecinestpasun.com as my personal blog. At the time it was a Django 1.0 site. I've used it intermittently, but never really paid any attention to it.

So, instead of persisting in the upkeep of a blog (and blog software) that is massive overkill for my small amount of publishing, I've moved the content over to [Lektor](https://getlektor.com), published as static content on [Github Pages](https://pages.github.com/). I've used Lektor for the [BeeWare](https://pybee.org) homepage, and it's proven very enjoyable to work with, and it allowed me to port over my old Django content in a morning.

I'm hoping to to a visual refresh in the near future; but in the meantime, I can at least save some money on hosting.
